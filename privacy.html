Privacy Policy

Strength Coach · Last updated September 23, 2026

Strength Coach ("the app") is published by [COMPANY LEGAL NAME] ("we"). This policy covers the iPhone and Android apps and the web version.

The short version

Everything you enter stays on your device. We don't have accounts, servers that receive your data, ads, analytics or tracking.

What the app stores, and where

To build and adjust your plan, the app saves the following only on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • Your quiz answers: goals, experience, household, work pattern, schedule, equipment, and anything you asked us to work around (such as knee pain or postpartum recovery) and your answer to the health check.
  • Your feedback after workouts, including exercises you marked as causing pain.
  • Daily check-ins (sleep quality and hunger) and the daily moves you tick off.
  • Creators you save or hide, videos you mark as done, and how many times you opened each creator's link.
  • Settings such as sound, voice and reminders.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, and we cannot recover it for you.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules them on your device using your phone's own notification system. No reminder information is sent to us.

Links to other services

Form videos, video ideas and creator suggestions open YouTube. When you follow a link, YouTube's own privacy policy applies to what you do there. We are not affiliated with the creators the app suggests.

The calendar option in the web version creates a calendar file on your device. What your calendar app does with it is governed by that app's policy.

The web version

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may keep technical logs such as IP addresses when pages are loaded; see GitHub's privacy statement. The app itself stores your data in your browser on your device, as described above.

Health information

Some of what you enter relates to your health (for example pain, postpartum recovery, sleep and hunger). It is used only on your device to tailor your plan and is never shared. The app provides general fitness guidance, not medical advice.

Children

The app is intended for adults. Some suggestions are activities to do with your kids, but the app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. In the web version, clearing your browser's site data for the app does the same. In either version, "Change my answers" and "Undo all feedback changes" in Settings let you change your plan without deleting everything.

Changes to this policy

If a future version works differently, for example if we add optional sharing of anonymous usage totals, we will update this policy first and ask for your permission in the app before anything is shared.
