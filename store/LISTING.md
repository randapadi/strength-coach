**Description** (4000 max):

```
Strength Coach builds a workout plan around your real life.

Answer a few quick questions: your goals, your schedule, who's at home, what equipment you have, and anything to work around, like knee pain or postpartum recovery. You get a weekly plan that fits the time you actually have, from 10 minutes to 45, with a timer, form cues and a video for every exercise.

MADE FOR BUSY WEEKS
• Pick the days and times that work: early mornings, lunch breaks, or after the kids are in bed.
• Every day has a 10-minute version for when things go sideways.
• Reminders before each workout, and optional nudges to take a short walk.

MOVE MORE WITHOUT TRYING HARDER
• Daily moves suggest easy ways to add activity: taking the stairs, walking calls, stroller walks, park days and family hikes on the weekend.
• Ideas change with your household and workdays, so parents of toddlers and busy professionals get different suggestions.

A PLAN THAT LEARNS
• After each workout, tell the app if it was too easy, about right or too hard. The next session adjusts.
• Mark an exercise as painful and it's swapped out.
• Quick sleep and hunger check-ins. After a rough night, the app offers the shorter session.

FOLLOW ALONG WITH CREATORS
• Get matched with popular YouTube fitness creators who fit your goals, level and schedule, including kid-friendly options and physical therapists for aches and pains.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account. No ads. No tracking. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

Strength Coach offers general fitness guidance, not medical advice. Check with your doctor before starting a new exercise program, especially if you are pregnant, recovering from birth or managing a health condition. Strength Coach is not affiliated with or endorsed by the creators it suggests.
```

**App Review notes** (for the reviewer):

```
No login is required. On first launch, answer the short quiz (any answers work) to generate a plan.
Native features: scheduled local reminders before each workout and optional walk nudges (Settings via the "Plan" button, or the prompt on the first workout screen); the app works fully offline.
All data is stored on the device only; the app has no server and collects no data.
The app gives general fitness guidance with clear disclaimers and a health check that directs users to a doctor when appropriate. External creator links open their public YouTube channels; we are not affiliated with them.
```
